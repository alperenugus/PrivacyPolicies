Privacy Policy for Lullaby: Baby & Bump Tracker

Effective Date: April 18, 2026
Last Reviewed: April 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lullaby: Baby & Bump Tracker (the
"App", "Lullaby", "we", "us", "our") handles your personal information.
Lullaby is designed to be offline-first and privacy-preserving: the
data you log stays on your device and — if you choose — inside your own
iCloud account. This document exists to describe, honestly and in
plain English, exactly what data is handled, why, and by whom.


1. Who is responsible for your data

The developer listed as the "Seller" on the App Store page is the data
controller for the purposes of this policy. For any question about
your data, about this policy, or to exercise the rights described in
section 9, you can contact us at:

  alperenugus@gmail.com


2. Data stored on your device

The App is built around Apple's SwiftData framework. Every entry you
create inside the App is written to private storage on your own
device, not to any server operated by us. This includes:

  - Baby profiles (name or nickname, date of birth, gender, blood
    type, allergies, photo)
  - Pregnancy information (nickname, due date, trimester notes, gender
    if set)
  - Feeding logs (breastfeeding sessions, bottles, solid foods,
    allergic-reaction flags)
  - Sleep logs (start/end times, quality, location, soothing method)
  - Diaper and potty logs (type, color, texture, rash flag, notes)
  - Medication doses, temperature readings, vaccine records
  - Growth measurements (weight, length, head circumference)
  - Developmental milestones (AAP-style checklists)
  - Appointments and reminders
  - Photos you add from the camera or photo library

None of the information above is transmitted to our servers because
we do not operate any servers for Lullaby.


3. iCloud sync (optional, controlled by you)

If you are signed into iCloud on your device, Lullaby uses Apple's
CloudKit framework to sync the data in section 2 across your own
devices, privately. This means:

  - Data is stored in your personal iCloud account, in the private
    database associated with your Apple ID.
  - We do not have access to the contents of your iCloud database.
  - Apple's own privacy policy applies to this processing:
    https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

If you do not want iCloud sync, sign out of iCloud, turn off iCloud
for the Lullaby app, or use Airplane Mode. The App functions fully
offline without iCloud.


4. Co-parent sharing (optional)

The App includes a "Co-parent sharing" feature that uses Apple's
CKShare technology to let another parent see the same baby timeline
as you. When you invite a co-parent:

  - The invitation is transmitted via Apple's iCloud sharing system.
  - The shared data lives in your private iCloud database; Apple
    grants the co-parent access via their Apple ID.
  - We do not see, store, or process the invitation or the shared
    data at any point.

You can stop sharing at any time from the in-app Co-parent Sharing
screen, or from iCloud settings on any of your Apple devices.


5. Subscriptions, in-app purchases, and grandfathering

Lullaby offers an optional subscription ("Lullaby Plus") and a
one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are handled by Apple's In-App
Purchase system. Apple processes the payment — we never see your card
number, Apple ID password, or billing address.

Two third-party services are used to manage subscription state:

  5.1. RevenueCat (entitlement management)
       Provider: RevenueCat, Inc. (USA)
       What it receives: an anonymous RevenueCat App User ID, your
         App Store purchase receipts, and the product identifier you
         purchased. It does not receive any of the baby/pregnancy
         data in sections 2–4.
       Why: to verify that your subscription is active across your
         devices, so the App can unlock Lullaby Plus features.
       Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

  5.2. Superwall (paywall presentation and experimentation)
       Provider: Superwall Labs, Inc. (USA)
       What it receives: an anonymous Superwall device identifier,
         paywall impression events, and whether you completed a
         purchase flow. It does not receive any of the baby/pregnancy
         data in sections 2–4.
       Why: to render paywall screens, run A/B tests on paywall
         copy, and avoid showing paywalls to users who already hold
         a subscription.
       Policy: https://superwall.com/privacy

Historical note: users who purchased the App at any point before
version 2.0 (when the App was sold for a one-time fee) have been
granted lifetime Lullaby Plus at no cost. This grant was executed
manually by the developer inside RevenueCat, using the original App
Store purchase receipt on each user's device. The underlying receipt
is part of Apple's In-App Purchase system and is handled by Apple.


6. Device permissions

The App will only request the permissions it needs. You can revoke
any of these at any time from the iOS Settings app.

  6.1. Notifications
       Used to remind you about upcoming feeds, nap windows, expiring
       medication doses, fever alerts, and appointments. If you deny
       notifications, the rest of the App works normally.

  6.2. Camera and Photo Library
       Used to let you attach a photo to a baby profile or to a
       memory in the in-app Gallery. Images are stored in local
       SwiftData storage (and your iCloud database if sync is on).
       We do not upload photos to any server.

  6.3. Siri / App Intents
       Used for voice shortcuts such as "Log a wet diaper in Lullaby".
       This uses Apple's on-device App Intents framework. Siri
       queries are processed by Apple, not by us, and we do not
       receive or store any audio.

The App does NOT request: location, contacts, microphone, Bluetooth,
HealthKit, Motion & Fitness, Media Library, or advertising
identifiers.


7. What we do not do

We want to be explicit about practices we do not engage in:

  - No third-party analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, no
    Mixpanel, no Amplitude, no Segment, no Meta/Facebook SDK, no
    TikTok SDK, no Google Analytics).
  - No advertising networks. No ads inside the App.
  - No behavioral profiling. We do not build a profile of you.
  - No data sold to third parties, ever.
  - No email collection. We do not have an email list.
  - No account creation. There is no Lullaby account; the App is
    tied to your Apple ID via iCloud only.
  - No App Tracking Transparency prompt. The App does not ask to
    track you across other apps because it does not track you.


8. Children's privacy

Lullaby contains health and developmental information about infants
and young children, but it is operated by the parent or legal
guardian on that adult's own device and under their own Apple ID.
The App does not create accounts for children, does not request data
from children directly, and does not serve advertising to anyone.

If you are under the age of 13 (or the equivalent age in your
jurisdiction) and have used the App on your own device, please ask a
parent or legal guardian to contact us and we will delete any
relevant data on request. (Because we do not operate a server, most
deletion happens by removing the App from the device.)


9. Your rights (GDPR, CCPA, KVKK, and others)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access,
correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your
personal data. Since we do not operate a server:

  - To access your data: open the App. Everything is there.
  - To export your data: use the in-app PDF report export (Settings
    → Export Report) or Apple's standard Data & Privacy export
    from your Apple ID account.
  - To correct your data: edit it inside the App.
  - To delete your data: delete entries in the App, remove the App
    from your device, or delete the App's data from iCloud via
    iOS Settings → your Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
  - To opt out of iCloud sync: turn off iCloud for the App in iOS
    Settings.
  - For subscription-related data: open Settings → Lullaby Plus →
    Manage Subscription, or write to us.

For anything else, email alperenugus@gmail.com and we will respond
within 30 days.


10. Security

Data stored on your device is protected by iOS's device-level
encryption. Data stored in iCloud is encrypted in transit and at
rest by Apple. RevenueCat and Superwall communicate over HTTPS with
TLS 1.2 or higher. We do not operate any servers, so the security of
your data ultimately depends on the security of your Apple ID and
your devices.


11. Data retention

Because we do not store your data, we do not retain it. Data lives on
your device (and, if enabled, in your iCloud account) until you
delete it. RevenueCat retains anonymous subscription records for as
long as your subscription is active, plus Apple's receipt-validation
window after cancellation. Superwall retains anonymous paywall
impression records per its own retention policy.


12. International transfers

RevenueCat and Superwall operate servers in the United States.
If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom,
or another region with data-transfer rules, your anonymous
subscription identifier and purchase events may be transferred to
the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent
safeguards provided by those vendors.
